Token Radar

Every pre-token protocol in the snapshot, ranked by what its token would be worth if it launched today.

How this radar works

Each row's implied value follows from the snapshot's own figures, never from a hand-picked list of names:

implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity × breadth

  • Category multiple. The median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the category's live-token protocols, shrunk toward the global median for thin categories. A category needs at least two live comparables to earn its own multiple; fewer, and the global median applies. No multiple exceeds 1.0× TVL: a launch is not valued above the value the protocol already holds.
  • Growth. The 7-day TVL change, clamped to −90%..+400% and square-root dampened, so a noisy week moves a row by at most half.
  • Maturity. 0.60 for a brand-new protocol, rising to 1.00 over three years. A protocol with no listing date is scored as one year old.
  • Breadth. A capped log factor for the number of chains: nothing for one chain, at most +15%.

Limits, stated plainly. An implied value is a model estimate, not a price and not a promise: a token may never launch, and if it does, the market sets the price. A protocol "records no token" in this snapshot; that is what the record says, not proof that no token exists. The farm plans are a starting point, not financial advice.
